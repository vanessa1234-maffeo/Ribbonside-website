2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Full name and email address
  • Date of account creation and plan type (free or Plus)

2.2 Health and Medical Data

  • Cancer type, stage, grade, Ki-67 score
  • Receptor status (ER, PR, HER2) and associated pathology detail
  • Tumour size, lymph node status, and surgical history
  • Diagnosis date, treating hospital, and oncologist details
  • Medications, doses, schedules, and dose log history
  • Symptom logs and wellbeing check-in scores
  • Blood results and tumour markers you enter

All health and medical data listed above is sensitive information under the Privacy Act 1988 and is handled under the highest standard of protection available under Australian law.

2.3 Documents and Photos

  • Photos you upload (treatment progress, skin changes, milestones)
  • Medical documents (pathology reports, referral letters, imaging reports)

2.4 Diary and Notes

  • Mood entries, diary text, and tags you write in the private diary
  • Notes from your support circle members

2.5 Appointments and Care Network

  • Appointment dates, times, locations, and clinician names
  • Healthcare team details you enter (name, hospital, phone, email)

2.6 Support Circle

  • Names, email addresses, and access levels of people you invite

2.7 Usage Data

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Features used and session duration (anonymised analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing the app: managing your health profile, medications, appointments, diary, and results
  • AI-powered features: processing typed questions for Ask AI explanations
  • Reminders: sending push notifications for medication alarms and appointment reminders
  • Support circle: enabling authorised supporters to view the data you choose to share with them
  • App improvement: analysing anonymised usage patterns to improve performance
  • Legal compliance: meeting our obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 and other applicable law

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Data residency: all personal and health data is stored on servers located in Australia (Sydney — AWS ap-southeast-2) via Supabase
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 on all data and files
  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 for all data transfers
  • Access controls: row-level security (RLS) ensures you can only access your own data
  • Photos and documents: stored in a private, encrypted storage bucket — not publicly accessible by URL

TGA Compliance & ACL Statement

Therapeutic Goods Administration · Australian Consumer Law · Annual Review Required

1. TGA Position — Software as a Medical Device

Ribbonside has been assessed against the TGA's guidance on Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) under the Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 (Cth).

Our position: Ribbonside does not meet the statutory definition of a medical device and is not required to be entered on the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG).

2. Why Ribbonside Is Not a Medical Device

A medical device must be intended to diagnose, prevent, monitor, treat, or alleviate a disease or condition. Ribbonside does not do any of these things:

  • It does not diagnose any medical condition
  • It does not recommend specific treatments or medications for a user's individual condition
  • It does not interpret clinical data to make individualised clinical decisions
  • Ask AI explains general medical terminology — it does not assess individual patients
  • Blood results tracking shows values the user enters — it does not advise whether those values require clinical action

3. Lines We Do Not Cross

If Ribbonside were to say "based on your Ki-67 you should consider X treatment" or "your blood results suggest you call your oncologist now" — that would constitute a medical device function. All features are explicitly designed to avoid this. The app tracks and informs. It never advises or decides.

4. TGA Advertising Compliance

Under the Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code, all Ribbonside marketing must:

  • Use words like "track", "log", "record", "store", "view" ✅
  • Not use "treat", "manage", "improve", "heal", "alleviate", "diagnose" ❌
  • Not claim therapeutic benefit
  • Not imply the app replaces professional medical care
  • Include the medical disclaimer prominently in the App Store listing

Correct: "Track your medications and log your appointments"
Incorrect: "Manage your breast cancer treatment and improve your outcomes"

5. Australian Consumer Law — Consumer Guarantees

The Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth)) provides automatic consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded by contract. For Ribbonside users, these guarantees mean:

  • Due care and skill: the app will be maintained to function as described and issues addressed within a reasonable timeframe
  • Fit for disclosed purpose: the app will perform the tracking, storage, and AI explanation functions described in the App Store listing
  • Reasonable time: service outages will be addressed promptly
  • Refund rights: where the Service fails a consumer guarantee, users may be entitled to a remedy — see the Refund & Cancellation tab

Nothing in our Terms of Service or any other Ribbonside document excludes or limits these non-excludable ACL rights. Contact support@ribbonside.com.au, NSW Fair Trading on 13 32 20, or the ACCC on 1300 302 502 to exercise your ACL rights.
