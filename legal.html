5. Local Storage And Overseas Processing

Your core health records, documents, photos, scans, notes, appointments, medications, symptoms, and diary entries are stored in Ribbonside's encrypted local vault on your device. Ribbonside does not use Supabase as the primary storage location for those records in this launch version.

Supabase stores limited online records needed for account sign-in, consent history, support, subscription entitlement, legal/security operations, and AI usage limits in Australia - Sydney (AWS ap-southeast-2). Trusted providers may process limited data where needed for billing, subscriptions, support, optional AI features, or legal and security purposes.

Because the health vault is local to your device, Ribbonside may not be able to recover your records if you delete the app, erase the vault, lose the phone, or cannot restore from your own backup or export.

For safeguards, support access, app lock, and incident response, see Security Practices.

Security Practices

Last updated: 25 May 2026 · Safeguards and incident response

1. What This Covers

This page summarises how Ribbonside protects personal information and health information. The Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, use, disclose, and delete. The Health Data Statement lists the health data categories handled by the app.

2. Safeguards

  • Core health records, documents, photos, scans, notes, appointments, medications, symptoms, and diary entries are stored in an encrypted local vault on your device.
  • Limited online account, consent, support, security, subscription, and AI usage-limit records are stored in Supabase in Australia - Sydney (AWS ap-southeast-2).
  • Data is encrypted in transit. Local vault data is encrypted on device in native builds, and online records are protected by Supabase security controls.
  • Supabase Row Level Security restricts online account data to the signed-in user and authorised service/admin access where needed for support, billing, legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes.
  • The app requires a PIN, with optional Face ID or Touch ID where supported by the device.
  • Admin and support access is limited to support, security, billing, legal, or abuse-prevention needs.

3. Support Access

Support tickets may include account details, app diagnostics, and any details you choose to send us. Support access is used only to investigate tickets, security issues, billing or account problems, suspected misuse, or legal obligations.

4. User Controls

  • Export your app data from the Account screen where available and keep exports secure.
  • Erase the local health vault on this device from the Account screen.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information by contacting support.
  • Delete your account and have online account records deleted or de-identified within 30 days, subject to limited legal, security, billing, or backup retention needs.
  • Revoke Apple Health access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete photos, documents, reports, diary entries, and other local app items you no longer want to keep.

5. Data Breach Response

Ribbonside maintains a data breach response plan. If we suspect an eligible data breach, we will take reasonable steps to assess it within 30 calendar days. If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected users and the OAIC as soon as practicable, including what happened, what information was involved, and recommended steps. If direct contact is not practicable, we may publish a notice on this website and take reasonable steps to publicise it.

6. Limits And Reporting

No app, device, or online service can guarantee perfect security. A stolen, unlocked, jailbroken, rooted, or compromised device may still expose local records. If you suspect a privacy or security issue involving Ribbonside, contact support@ribbonside.com.au.

TGA Compliance & ACL Statement

Therapeutic Goods Administration · Australian Consumer Law

1. TGA Position

Ribbonside has been assessed against the TGA's guidance on Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) under the Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 (Cth). Ribbonside does not meet the statutory definition of a medical device and is not required to be entered on the ARTG.

2. Why Ribbonside Is Not a Medical Device

  • It does not diagnose any medical condition
  • It does not recommend specific treatments or medications for a user's individual condition
  • It does not interpret clinical data to make individualised clinical decisions
  • Ask AI explains general medical terminology — it does not assess individual patients

3. TGA Advertising Compliance

Ribbonside marketing should stay in conservative personal-organiser language such as "track", "log", "record", "store", "organise", and "remind". It must not claim to diagnose, treat, monitor, prevent, cure, improve, or manage a disease or treatment pathway, and it must not make therapeutic benefit claims.

The Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth)) provides automatic consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded by contract. Nothing in our Terms excludes these non-excludable rights. Contact support@ribbonside.com.au or NSW Fair Trading on 13 32 20.
