Account and data deletion

Delete your account or local data.

You can erase local health records while keeping your account, delete individual records, or delete your Ribbonside account.

Delete data without deleting your account

  1. Open Ribbonside.
  2. Go to Account > Privacy & Your Data.
  3. Choose Erase Health Vault to delete local health records from that device while keeping your Ribbonside account.
  4. Individual records can also be deleted from their relevant sections.

This deletes local-vault content from that device, including local health records, documents, photos, appointments, medications, notes, questions, symptoms and other local records where applicable.

Your online account, consent, subscription, support, security and AI usage-limit records remain unless you delete your account or make a separate deletion request. The retention explanation below still applies.

Account or online data deletion request support@ribbonside.com.au

Use the subject line Ribbonside account deletion request and email from the address used for your Ribbonside account where possible.

To request deletion of Ribbonside-controlled online data while keeping your account, use the subject line Ribbonside data deletion request and tell us which data you want deleted. Local-vault data can only be erased from the device because Ribbonside cannot access it remotely.

We may need to verify your identity before deleting online account records.

Delete from inside the app

Open Ribbonside, go to Account, choose Delete account, then follow the confirmation steps. The app asks you to type DELETE before it sends the deletion request.

Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled Ribbonside or cannot access the app, email support with your deletion request. Include enough information for us to identify your account, but do not send Medicare numbers, medical record numbers, full reports, or unnecessary health details.

What is deleted

When deletion succeeds in the app, Ribbonside erases the local health vault on that device and deletes or de-identifies Ribbonside-controlled online account, consent, support, subscription, security, and AI usage-limit records where reasonably possible.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained where needed for legal, security, billing, dispute, backup, audit, abuse-prevention, or service-integrity reasons. Provider logs and backups may persist until their normal cycle expires.

Deleting your Ribbonside account or deleting the app does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel active subscriptions through the store where you subscribed.
Ribbonside is a local-first app. Core health records, documents, and photos are stored in the encrypted local vault on your device. If you delete the app, erase the vault, lose the device, or cannot restore from your own backup/export, Ribbonside may not be able to recover those local records.
