Why I built Ribbonside

Built from lived experience.

I was diagnosed with breast cancer in September 2025. What followed was multiple surgeries, chemotherapy, radiation, and now hormone blockers.

During treatment, I felt completely overwhelmed by the practical side: appointments, medications, questions, results, photos and documents everywhere.

I ended up building something for myself called Ribbonside, just to help keep that side of breast cancer in one place.

I hope you find it as useful as I did.

Built for practical organisation.

Private by design

Core records stay in the on-device vault. Limited online records support sign-in, consent, support, subscriptions, and AI usage limits.

Plain-English help

Optional What does this mean? is limited to short typed questions and general explanations. It is not a personalised medical assistant.

Clear user control

Delete local records, export your data, manage subscriptions through Apple, and revoke Apple Health access any time.
