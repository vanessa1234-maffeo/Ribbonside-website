Everything you need

Your whole journey,
finally organised

From diagnosis day to your last treatment session — Ribbonside holds it all, so you don't have to carry it in your head.

🔬

Understand Your Diagnosis

Your diagnosis profile — cancer type, stage and grade — explained in plain language. Log your diagnosis details and tap to understand what each result means.

💊

Medication Tracking

Log every dose, set medication alarms, and see your full dose history at a glance. Colour-coded by type — chemotherapy, hormone therapy, supplements and more.

🤖

Ask AI — In Plain English

What is Ki-67? What does triple negative mean? What are the side effects of Tamoxifen? Get clear, compassionate answers from an AI that knows breast cancer terminology.

👥

My Circle

Invite your partner, mum, or daughter to see exactly what they're allowed to see. Control access to your health data — Full, View Only, or Limited. Carers leave you notes and messages.

📓

Private Diary

A private journal just for you. Log how you're feeling, tag by symptom, mood, or milestone. Your diary is yours — only visible to circle members you choose to share with.

📅

Appointments

Track every upcoming and past appointment with your oncologist, radiation team, GP and pathology lab. Colour-coded, filterable, and synced with your iPhone calendar.
